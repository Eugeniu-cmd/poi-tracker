POI Tracker — Settings

v0.27 — 2026-10-30. Settings on the canon after the user's five decisions: the gear opens it (item 73), a city is put in use from its page (item 21), a route has its own page and loads from the sheet when it is added (item 22), and the dialogs name the passes they would lose (items 18, 24). Every value inside the frames is a token of _tokens.css v0.24 and every rule follows screens.md v0.62. The shell (top bar, POI column, presets, drag corner, light and dark) is that of main-screen.html v0.44, copied unchanged: presets 800 × 1333 (the user's tablet in portrait, the default), 720 × 1000, 1333 × 800 and 1000 × 600. Every frame also drags at its bottom-right corner. In portrait Settings is one column that drills down, in landscape the list stays beside the page (item 74). Ten states, A to J. Graz, Linz and the R20891 codes are sample data. Every text in the sheet reads at 4.5:1 or more in both themes (v0.10). The consistency audit added frame J, the kept route code of frame E and the POI types in the two dialogs (v0.11). The compass follows main-screen v0.29 (v0.12, v0.13). A red button that loses passes reads "Erase anyway" or "Delete anyway", and every button takes the size of the action bar's buttons (v0.14). The compass arrow is always on the dial (v0.15). A POI in progress is grey with a dark bar (v0.16). The type pill's numbers are gold (v0.17). Metallic gold replaces the amber, with gold arrows, a gold ring on a disc that can be tapped and a gold N on the compass (v0.18). Discs keep their centre when they scale, and a compass with no heading draws a dashed arrow (v0.19). The "Still needed" card shows rings and a rose, and the type pill's numbers are in ink with "Change type". Every arrow, ring and bar of a POI in progress takes one gold (v0.20), now lighter (v0.21). The shell carries the rules of decision D1, passes to fix, which no frame here shows, and the Sound switch's rules, which the type change dialog of main-screen now shares (v0.22). The shell of main-screen v0.40, and with it the shell of v0.39, which Canon D2 did not copy here, brings the card at scale 3, the full-size plus and the side rules of decision D2, and the colours of emerald and warm gold through the tokens: every frame shows the working screen behind Settings with the card and the discs, the arrows on the ivory discs of the dark theme in a deep gold and the card's icons in the bright gold (--card-glyph). No frame here shows a Straight only POI (v0.23). The shell of main-screen v0.41 draws the arrows of the type pill's split and of a removal dialog's chip in the gold of the card's icons, --card-glyph: every frame shows the pill behind Settings, none a removal dialog. History's own rows keep the arrow in the text colour (v0.24). The shell of main-screen v0.42 gives a done square of the mini-map, the done tiles of the grid and the legend's Done swatch --ok-tile on --ok-tile-bg, on light a brighter green than the column's --ok: the mini-map above the column shows it behind Settings, the grid no frame here shows (v0.25). The shell of main-screen v0.43 gives a locked disc no shadow, since the user's tablet drew a band of garbage across the screen under a half-transparent disc with a blurred shadow: every frame shows the working screen behind Settings with its three discs, none of them locked (v0.26). The shell of main-screen v0.44 makes the panels solid: --panel, --panel-strong and --panel-edge are opaque, the colours the translucent ones gave over --bg, since the user's tablet drew see-through panels too light and made them blink, flip and veil, and the mini-map, the rail and the bar's buttons take the new --panel-inset: every frame shows the working screen behind Settings with them, the sheet itself on --panel-raised (v0.27).

E · A route

Decision map
  • 22 Its own page: "Route code" and "Number of POIs", the hint on the tab's name, "Save".
  • 22, 26, 27 "Route code" stays, dimmed, with its reason: R20890-058 is a checked route and its 2 waiting passes carry this code, and events are never edited (data-model.md). A tab not found is renamed in the sheet instead (item 47). A change not accepted holds the code the same way.
