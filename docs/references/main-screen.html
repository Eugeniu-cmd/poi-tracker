POI Tracker — working screen

v0.39 — 2026-10-25. The card at scale 3: the rings keep their size in columns of 90px, Straight sits one and a half rings above Left and Right, the diamonds grow to the rings' height, and the space before, between and after the pyramid and the rose is equal on the drawn shapes (item 39). The over ring's plus is as big as the digits and on their middle line, so "+26" reads as one number. On a Straight only POI each side shows its passes against 20, in the done colour at 20 and as "+3 over" past it, and nothing locks (decision D2, items 39, 46). New frame U: POI 152 with 20 passes from North and 14 from South. Decision D1: a POI is done when every ring is closed. The counter counts the passes that fit the type, and passes that do not fit are "to fix": in the rest line and the summary, on the row as a warning triangle before "N to go" or as the tag "to fix", in the caption under the locked discs and on the grid's tiles and legend (items 3, 4, 40, 46, 59). The over ring reads "+26 over" with the number a step smaller, centred (item 39). The type change dialog says that the sheet keeps the passes and the export sends them, and offers to delete this tablet's own with a switch (items 63, 64, frame I). New frames S and T show POI 131 with its rings open and POI 141 with its rings closed, both with 53 passes to fix. The one gold of the arrows, the rings and the bars is lighter, #CCA735 (item 5). The "Still needed" card, headed TO GO, shows the maneuvers as gold rings in a pyramid and the sides as a rose of four diamonds (item 39), and the type pill's button reads "Change type" (item 60). Every maneuver arrow, the ring of a disc and the bar of a POI in progress take one gold, --gold-line (item 5). The card's head shows the last pass of the POI ("LAST"), and the type pill and the type menu show no passes ("70 passes"), and the pill centres its name and split. A compass with no heading draws a dashed arrow (item 19, frame Q), the type change dialog's "Change" is red (item 63, frame I), and discs keep their centre when they scale. Metallic gold replaces the amber. The arrows on the maneuver discs and on the type tile are gold, a disc that can be tapped wears a gold ring, and the compass letter N is gold (items 5, 19). The type pill's passes and split are larger and in ink, still smaller than the card's counts (item 60). The POI states read at a glance, also under sun glare: a POI in progress is grey with a gold bar, and done, sheet error and not drivable carry a sign besides their colour (items 58, 59). Dialog buttons take the size of the action bar's buttons (item 63). The compass is one arrow on a raised round face, its left half dark like the maneuver discs and its right half gold like the side discs, with the dial fixed and N up. The arrow is always there: with no heading yet it shows the course the tablet saved last, or north, as a dashed outline (item 19). Every value inside the frames is a token of _tokens.css v0.21 (size C, screens.md item 67) and every rule follows screens.md v0.54. Icons are Lucide only, one symbol per maneuver (item 69). The board is rubber (item 54): presets 800 × 1333 (the user's tablet in portrait, the default), 720 × 1000 (portrait minimum), 1333 × 800 (landscape) and 1000 × 600 (landscape minimum); every frame also drags at its bottom-right corner. The POI column is wide from 1200px and the main area follows the frame's shape: side by side when wider than tall, stacked when taller (item 51). Below 700px of height the compact step applies: smaller display numbers, a one-line grid button instead of the mini-map and, in landscape, narrower margins and gaps so the card keeps its paddings (item 70). Twenty-one states, A to U.

Preset Theme Checking tool only — nothing here is part of the app.
