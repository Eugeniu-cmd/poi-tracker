POI Tracker — working screen

v0.18 — 2026-10-06. Redrawn from scratch on the canon: every value inside the frames is a token of _tokens.css v0.6 (size C, screens.md item 67) and every rule follows screens.md v0.28. The board is rubber (item 54): presets 800 × 1333 (the user's tablet in portrait, the default), 720 × 1000 (portrait minimum), 1333 × 800 (landscape) and 1000 × 600 (landscape minimum); every frame also drags at its bottom-right corner. The POI column switches at 1000px (item 51). Fourteen states, A to N. Open question at 1000 × 600: with the wide column, the main area keeps only 204px beside the 382px disc cluster for the number, the type pill and the card, which need about 330px; that frame shows the conflict as drawn, for a decision.

Preset Theme Checking tool only — nothing here is part of the app.
