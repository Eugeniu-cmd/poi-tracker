POI Tracker — working screen

v0.20 — 2026-10-09. Fixes from the audit of v0.19: every value inside the frames is a token of _tokens.css v0.8 (size C, screens.md item 67) and every rule follows screens.md v0.30. Icons are Lucide only, one symbol per maneuver (item 69). The board is rubber (item 54): presets 800 × 1333 (the user's tablet in portrait, the default), 720 × 1000 (portrait minimum), 1333 × 800 (landscape) and 1000 × 600 (landscape minimum); every frame also drags at its bottom-right corner. The POI column is wide from 1200px and the main area follows the frame's shape: side by side when wider than tall, stacked when taller (item 51). Fourteen states, A to N.

Preset Theme Checking tool only — nothing here is part of the app.

Decision map

  • 59 Six tile states, as the mini-map: current, done, in progress, not started, sheet error, not drivable; each tile and swatch carries its state on the border.
